BORs are next week 2/10 come prepared with requirements done if you need a scout master conference this week. For those of you that don’t know, BOR stands for board of review. This is a panel of parents that will ask you a couple questions about rank advancement or your favorite thing that happened during your time of advancement. If you sign up for a BOR you should have all the requirements for that rank done besides the actual BOR. There is no need to be nervous; it’s all friendly faces asking basic questions.
